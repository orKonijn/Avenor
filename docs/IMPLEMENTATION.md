## Operational assumptions

- SQLite is a single-server development persistence layer. WAL mode and synchronous transactions make revision/idempotency/audit writes atomic in this process. Horizontal deployment requires a database and concurrency strategy appropriate to multiple instances.
- Sample sessions expire after seven days. Expired session/token rows are cleaned periodically; demo workspace retention needs an operational policy before a public demo is exposed at scale.
- In the development UI, `ng serve` proxies `/v1` to the Express listener. The production Express server serves the built SPA. Critical CSS inlining is disabled so the build does not require inline script exceptions to CSP.
- Initial fixture identity, prices, chart values, and theme contents are synthetic. They are not investment recommendations or live market observations.
- Portfolio and whole-workspace revisions prevent stale transaction saves and stale UI snapshots. Idempotency replays return the original operation result together with the current authoritative workspace.
- Missing display FX suppresses a total and gains rather than silently falling back to an exchange rate of one. Cross-currency cost basis cannot be inferred from current FX.

## Before a production release

Complete the provider and identity boundaries above, license the data universe, implement reliable alert ingestion and email delivery, establish retention/backup policies, resolve the remaining MVP coverage, and measure performance/security/accessibility in the actual deployment. No release tag or public deployment is part of this initial GitHub upload.

Opening holdings establish shares without a cash debit. Unknown basis propagates to unavailable gains, including realized gains for sales consuming unknown-basis lots. Daily-loss sample alerts use consecutive UTC dates and flow-adjusted close-to-close returns, not peak-relative drawdown. Synthetic observation controls do not change ledger valuations.

History requests cover at most 366 UTC dates and include the preceding close for return calculations. Aggregate history includes archived portfolios' past activity, while current aggregate totals exclude them. Opening holdings contribute their closing market value (adjusted to closing share units for later same-day splits); acquisition cost is not a cash flow. Daily TWR is unavailable across missing values, zero starting values, or invalid subperiod factors. Period monetary gain may remain available with complete endpoints and flows despite intermediate quote gaps. No quotes are carried forward. Net flows of at least 10% of prior value receive a timing warning. Intraday offsetting gross flows, cross-currency transfers, market calendars, and licensed history remain future work.

Transfers save or void two linked cash legs atomically after replaying both portfolios. Individual legs cannot be edited independently. Revisions guard both accounts, and idempotency protects uncertain retries. Aggregate history excludes only a complete, active, matching pair; each individual portfolio treats its leg as external flow. Transfer fees and currency conversions are separate transactions.
